# Silent demo script

No voice. Every screen says what is happening; consensus waits are cut short with the transaction link left on screen.
Use two browser profiles, one wallet each (call them Kiln and Sera), both on Studio Next with test GEN from the site's
faucet.

| Time | Screen | What the viewer sees |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Landing, https://usesamepage.vercel.app | "Agree on the deal before the work starts." and the agreed deal card, term by term |
| 0:08 | Open a page (Kiln) | Title "Kiln launch posts", Sera's address, 20 GEN deposit, 48 h windows; the link to send |
| 0:14 | The page (Sera) | **Join with 20 GEN** |
| 0:18 | Write and seal, both | Two different accounts typed; "Terms your text mentions" fills in; only hashes visible to the other side |
| 0:32 | Open, then check | **Open my account** twice, then **Check round 1**: "Validators are comparing…", then **Different · 2**, deliverable and payment hatched, side by side |
| 0:50 | Round 2 | "Round 1 differed on DELIVERABLE, PAYMENT"; both rewrite, seal, open, check: **Same page** |
| 1:05 | Sign | **Sign the record** twice: Agreed, deposits back; the agreed record page; **Withdraw** on My pages |

Texts that reproduce the run (page 14 on chain used these):

- Kiln, round 1: "Sera writes 3 posts and a thread on X about the Kiln launch, all live by Oct 24, 2026. Kiln pays 600 USDC, all of it after the last post is live."
- Sera, round 1: "I post 3 times on X about Kiln's launch, all live by Oct 24, 2026, for 600 USDC, half before I start and half after the last post."
- Kiln, round 2: "Sera writes 3 posts and one thread on X about the Kiln launch, all live by Oct 24, 2026. Kiln pays 600 USDC, half before Sera starts and half after the thread is live. Kiln may repost and quote the posts."
- Sera, round 2: "I write 3 posts and a thread on X about Kiln's launch, everything live by October 24, 2026. 600 USDC: half before I start, the other half once the thread is live. Kiln can repost and quote them."
