## Is it a contract?

It is a record both sides wrote and signed, on chain and timestamped. Whether it binds anyone is a question for the
people and the place involved; Samepage only shows that both described the same deal.

## Who sees my account?

Nobody until both have sealed. Once both open, everyone.

## Can I change my account after sealing?

Not in the same round: one seal per side per round, and the open must match it. If the round comes back different, round
2 is a fresh seal.

## What if I lose my ticket?

Load the downloaded file on the page. Without the text and salt the seal cannot be opened, and missing the opening window
loses the deposit.

## What if they ghost?

Wait for the window to close, then press **Close as no-show**. Their deposit is credited to you; withdraw it from
[My pages](/me).

## Why two rounds and not more?

Two rounds catch the misunderstanding and give both a chance to fix it. A third would let a side ask the same question
until the answer suits. If round 2 still differs, the deal is not agreed, and nobody loses money for saying so.

## Can one side's text tell the judge what to say?

It can try. Both accounts are fenced as data before they reach the model: every `<` and `>` is replaced, so no text can
close its own block, and the prompt says to ignore any instruction inside the accounts. Golden case 8 puts an instruction
in an account; its result is in the [evaluation](/docs/more/evaluation).

## Which network?

GenLayer Studio Next, chain 61997. See [Addresses and network](/docs/reference/addresses).
