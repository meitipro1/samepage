---
title: Limitations
description: What Samepage does not do, and the honest limits of a deal check.
---

- **It checks agreement, not fairness.** Two accounts can describe the same bad deal. Samepage says they match.
- **It holds no payment and judges no delivery.** The deposit protects the steps of the check, not the work. Whether the
  posts went live or the files arrived is outside the record.
- **Everything is public once opened.** Both accounts, the statuses and the notes are on chain next to the addresses.
  Use it for deals that can be public, such as promotions, gigs and collaborations, not confidential ones.
- **Six terms, not every clause.** A deal with a term outside the six (a non-compete, say) is compared only where that
  clause touches one of the six.
- **The model can be wrong.** Validators agree on its six labels exactly, asked in both orders, but agreement is not
  truth. Both sides read both texts before signing, which is why the texts, not the labels, are the record. The
  [evaluation](/docs/more/evaluation) publishes the misses.
- **One address is not one person.** Samepage binds each side to an address, not an identity.
- **Test network.** It runs on GenLayer Studio Next with test GEN.
