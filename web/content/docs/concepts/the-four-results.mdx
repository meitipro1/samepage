title: The four results
description: Same page, different, gaps and no show, what each one means and what it does with the deposits.
---

The model returns six statuses. The contract computes the verdict from them, so the rule for agreeing is code, not
wording, and the same page always means the same thing on every page.

```python
differ = [t for t in TERMS if status[t] == "DIFFER"]
gaps = [t for t in REQUIRED if status[t] == "ABSENT"]   # amount, deliverable, deadline
verdict = "DIFFERENT" if differ else ("GAPS" if gaps else "SAME_PAGE")
```

| Result | Meaning | Money | Record |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Same page** | Every term matches or is absent from both, and amount, deliverable and deadline are stated | Deposits back when both sign | An agreed record with both texts |
| **Different · N** | At least one term is stated differently, or by only one side | Deposits stay for round 2 | The differing terms, side by side |
| **Gaps · N** | No differences, but amount, deliverable or deadline is missing from both | Deposits stay for round 2 | The missing terms named |
| **No show** | A side missed a window to join, seal, open or sign | The absent side's deposit goes to the other | Recorded against the address |

## Two rounds at most

A page has at most two rounds. After round 1 comes back different or with gaps, both sides talk it through and each
writes and seals again; the round 1 accounts stay on the page. If round 2 is not the same page either, the page closes as
**not agreed** and both deposits come back, because disagreeing honestly is not ghosting.

## What N counts

For **Different**, N is the number of terms that differ. For **Gaps**, N is the number of required terms neither side
states. A page that differs on payment and leaves out the deadline reads **Different · 1**: differences come first, and
round 2 is the time to add the deadline too.
