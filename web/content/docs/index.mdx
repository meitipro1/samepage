Samepage is a deal check on GenLayer. The two sides of a deal, a project and a creator, a client and a freelancer, two
traders, each write the deal in their own words without seeing the other's. GenLayer validators compare the two accounts
on six terms and say exactly where they match, where they differ and what neither mentions. Both sides fix the
misunderstanding before the work starts, sign a record that holds both texts, and get back a good-faith deposit that a
side who walks away loses to the other.

## The problem it fixes

Most crypto deals, a paid thread, a logo, a landing page, an OTC trade, an ambassador role, are agreed in a few DMs, and
each side walks away with a different deal in their head. "3 posts" or "3 posts and a thread", "paid after" or "half
upfront", "by Friday" or "next Friday". Nobody notices until the work is delivered, and then it is a fight.

- **Nobody frames the deal.** There is no summary for one side to write and the other to skim. Both write their own
  account, and the record holds both.
- **Nobody copies.** Both accounts are sealed by hash until both are in, and a seal includes the writer's address, so a
  seal copied from the other side can never be opened.
- **Nobody picks the judge.** Either side could run a model on the two texts, then run it again until it agreed. On
  GenLayer several validators compare the accounts the same way, and the result is a record neither side controlled.
- **Nobody ghosts for free.** Every step has a window. A side that misses one loses its deposit to the other.

## Open, seal, open, check, sign

<Steps>
  <Step>**Open.** One side opens a page with a title, the counterparty's address, a deposit and a window per step. The other joins with the same deposit.</Step>
  <Step>**Seal.** Each side writes the deal in its own words and seals a hash of it.</Step>
  <Step>**Open.** When both have sealed, both open their accounts, each checked against its seal.</Step>
  <Step>**Check.** Validators compare the two accounts on six terms. The verdict follows in code.</Step>
  <Step>**Sign.** On the same page both sign and both deposits come back. Otherwise each side rewrites once.</Step>
</Steps>

## What Samepage is not

- **Not an escrow.** It holds a good-faith deposit, never the payment for the work.
- **Not a lawyer.** It checks that two accounts agree, not that the deal is fair or legal.
- **Not a dispute court.** It runs before the work. A dispute service can use the record afterwards.
- **Not private.** Both accounts are public once opened, so it suits deals that can be public.
