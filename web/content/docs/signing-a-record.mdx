---
title: Signing an agreed record
description: What a signature covers, when deposits come back, and how to share the record.
---

A round that comes back **Same page** moves the page to signing, with a fresh window. Each side presses **Sign the
record** once. When both have signed the page is **Agreed**, and both deposits are credited back to their balances.

## What you sign

You sign the record: both full accounts of the round that was checked, and its six statuses. You do not sign the notes.
The notes are one line per term from the leader validator, stored as a reading aid; they are never compared between
validators and never part of the record, so a misleading note cannot become part of the deal.

## If the other side does not sign

The window is the page's window per step. If it closes with one signature, anyone may call no-show: the side that did not
sign loses its deposit to the side that did. If neither signed, each gets their own back. See
[Deposits and no-shows](/docs/concepts/deposits-and-no-shows).

## The record page

An agreed page has a record at `/p/<id>/record`: the six statuses, the verdict computed from them, both accounts with
their seals, the times each was sealed, opened and signed, and the contract it was read from. Print it or save it as a
PDF, or share the link. Anyone can read it without a wallet, and anyone can check it against the chain with `get_page`.

## Withdrawing

Signing, a closed round 2 and a no-show only move balances inside the contract. **Withdraw** on [My pages](/me) is the
only call that pays: one top-level transfer of everything credited to you, on every page. The transfer settles at
finality, a little after the transaction is accepted.
